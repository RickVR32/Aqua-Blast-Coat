Services

Powder coating

Dry powder is sprayed on and baked into a hard, even finish that stands up to chips and wear better than most paint.

  • Gloss, satin and matt finishes
  • A wide range of colours
  • Metal items up to [MAX ITEM SIZE]

Sandblasting

Abrasive blasting strips rust, old paint and coatings back to bare metal, and gives the new finish a clean surface to grip.

  • Rust and scale removal
  • Old paint and powder coat stripped
  • Blast only, or blast then coat

Aqua blasting

Fine media carried in water cleans and finishes metal more gently than dry blasting, with no dust and a smooth, even surface.

  • Suits aluminium and delicate parts
  • Cleans without harsh surface damage
  • Aqua blast only, or aqua blast then coat
